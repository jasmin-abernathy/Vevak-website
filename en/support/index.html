Voluntary support · no required reward

Help VeVak stay free and open.

VeVak is developed as an open-source project, with no advertising and no telemetry. If the project is useful to you, or if you simply want to help fund the time spent building it, you can contribute an amount of your choice.

  • Choose your amount
  • No subscription
  • No feature unlock
  • Open-source project

What your support funds

Development time, not privileged access.

Contributions help cover part of the time and costs involved in development, real-device testing, documentation and project maintenance.

  • VeVak remains usable without contributing.
  • Supporting the project does not unlock a “better” version of the safety baseline.
  • There is no automatic subscription or recurring commitment.
  • Support does not qualify for a tax deduction.

Simple questions

What supporting VeVak changes — and what it does not.

Does VeVak become paid?

No. This page only makes it possible to support the project voluntarily. Paying nothing does not block access to VeVak.

Do I unlock anything?

No. Support does not purchase features, a special status or priority in the development roadmap.

Why contribute then?

Because a free project still takes time: development, testing, bug fixing, documentation, hosting and maintenance.

Another way to help

Feedback and testing matter too.

If you do not want to contribute financially, testing VeVak, reporting an issue or improving the documentation helps the project just as much.
